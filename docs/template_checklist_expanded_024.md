```
{{Documentation subpage}}
;Description
: This template is used to describe a <u>temporary</u> discount on [[champion]]s and [[Champion skin|skins]] made available by [[Riot Games Inc.]] For every item in sale, the template will display a splash art, the name of the item and its discounted and original price. The template support up to 30 items concurrently on sale. 

Sales that have yet to start will get automatically titled as Next sales, in progress sales will be titled Current sales and expired sales will be automatically hidden and placed in the [[:Category:Expired sale|Expired sale]] category.

;Usage
:Type <code>{{t|Sale box|...}}</code> on an article.

;Sample output
<pre>{{Sale box
|startdate = January 7th, 2020
|enddate   = January 14th, 2020

|champ1 = Kindred

|champ2 = Riven

|champ3 = Ahri
|cost3 = (Equals blank means its 50%)
```
