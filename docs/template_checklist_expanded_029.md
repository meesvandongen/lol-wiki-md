```
{{Documentation subpage}}
;Usage
This is a shorthand template for linking to articles on the {{w|English Wikipedia}}, outside of the League of Legends Wiki.

;Syntax
To simultaneously link to an English Wikipedia article and show the article name, the template syntax is:
 <nowiki>{{w|article title}}</nowiki>
To link to an article by name but showing different inline text while linking to it, the template syntax is:
 <nowiki>{{w|article title|different text}}</nowiki>
To link the Wikipedia article of non-English language, add the {{w|ISO 639-1}} language code then a colon before the article name:
 <nowiki>{{w|ja:Japanese article title|proper English translation of the title}}</nowiki>
 <nowiki>{{w|de:German article title|proper English translation of the title}}</nowiki>
or use the "lang" parameter to bypass redirect via English Wikipedia:
 <nowiki>{{w|lang=ja|Japanese article title|proper English translation of the title}}</nowiki>
 <nowiki>{{w|lang=de|German article title|proper English translation of the title}}</nowiki>

;See also
* {{t|fandom}}, link to an article of other Fandom project.
* {{t|wikipedia}}, which does NOT redirect here because it has a different function.

```
