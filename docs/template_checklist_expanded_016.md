```
{{Documentation subpage}}
<!-- Categories and interwikis go at the bottom of this page. -->
;Purpose
This template is for use in various places, to display a summary of the mechanics of a {{tipLoR|Region}}. It's supposed to give a brief idea of what a region is about, rather than a more in-depth explanation, that, in time, should be on the pages for the regions. At the time of writing, {{tipLoR|Bilgewater}} is the only region with any semblance of this.

The way it was judged best to summarise the mechanics was to split them into 3 tiers: {{sbc|Primary}}, {{sbc|Secondary}}, and {{sbc|Tertiary}}. ''Primary'' mechanics are presented as {{sbc|Main mechanics}}, and the others as {{sbc|Lesser mechanics}}, with the ''Tertiary'' ones in <small>''Small italics with small icons''</small>.
;Notes
A mechanic being listed here doesn't always just require it to just be present - this takes into account the relative prevalence within other regions, the amount of other cards that work with the mechanic in that region and others, etcetera.
For example, {{tipLoR|draw}} is something that every region has, it's core to the nature of the game, so there's a higher barrier to card draw being included here - for example the {{tipLoR|Shadow Isles}} don't mention it at all despite them having a {{LoR|Glimpse Beyond|spell}} that can draw cards, while it's integral to much of {{tipLoR|Piltover & Zaun}}, so it's mentioned as a major mechanic. Another good example of a decision is {{tipLoR|Silence}} and {{tipLoR|Capture}} - they are lesser mechanics for {{tipLoR|Demacia}} despite for the longest time it being the only region with them, as they are keywords with only one {{tipLoR|Spell}} each, and aren't designed as mechanics with any synergy in the region, or any connection to the rest of the region's '''gameplay''', despite their importance to the thematic. Them being made more prevalent in {{tipLoR|Bilgewater}} and {{tipLoR|Targon}} cemented their position. Currently, if Lesser=false, these two are shown in the main mechanics for Demacia, though how good an idea that is up for conversation.
;Making Changes
If you are changing this template, please justify the change in the edit summary, and if you are looking for a justification for things here, check the edit history first. Polite discussion about the decisions made here is encouraged, the [https://discord.gg/tBtHQc6 Discord Server] is the best place to discuss things.
;Syntax
<pre>{{Lrf|Noxus}} OR {{Lrf|NX}}</pre>
{{Lrf|NX}}

<pre>{{Lrf|Demacia|Text can be changed|Text can also be changed}} OR {{Lrf|DE|Text can be changed}}</pre>
{{Lrf|DE|Text can be changed|Text can also be changed}}

<pre>{{Lrf|Shadow Isles|notext|notext}} OR {{Lrf|SI|notext|notext}}</pre>
{{Lrf|SI|notext|notext}}
```
