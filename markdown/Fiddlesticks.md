### Passive: A Harmless Scarecrow

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|------:|
| **Effect Radius** | 900 (Reveal radius, center to edge) units |
| **Targeting** | Vector |
| **Affects** | Self |

**INNATE - SCARECROW EFFIGY:** **Fiddlesticks** begins the game with an exclusive Scarecrow Effigy, which permanently occupies the trinket slot. **Fiddlesticks** can pretend to be an *Effigy* by standing still and not acting nor being acted upon for 2 seconds, during which it adjusts its body into a scarecrow and extends its arm out to expose its lantern.

**INNATE - A HARMLESS SCARECROW:** From experience 6 onward, placing an Effigy also summons a Sweeper Drone at the location for 6 seconds.

**Notes:**

- Scarecrow Effigy are unavailable on Howling Abyss until **Fiddlesticks** uses or sells its Poro-Snax.
- The process of or the act of pretending to be an *Effigy* will be interrupted if **Fiddlesticks**:
  - Moves.
  - Takes damage.
  - Declares an attack.
  - Casts any ability or spell.
  - Starts channeling.
  - Is hit by an ability.
  - Is affected by a crowd control effect.
- *Effigies* will mimic **Fiddlesticks' ** idle animations and their health bars are hidden until they fake an action.
  - If **Fiddlesticks** pretends to be an *Effigy*, its health bar stays hidden until the pose is interrupted.
- Effigies are considered clones.
  - They are not valid targets to direction, ground, and auto-targeted effects for first $1.5$ seconds (Estimated) of their lifespan.
- The trinket slot can still be temporarily replaced by Eye of the Herald.
- **Fiddlesticks** can still purchase Support Quest items, as they do not affect the trinket slot.
- - Fiddlesticks has a special icon for this ability.

- *Terrify* will still cast and affect the target even if **Fiddlesticks** dies or loses sight of them during the cast time.
  - *Terrify* will still cast but not affect the target if they die or become untargetable during the cast time. There is no compensation.
- The player's screen will have a visual effect when successfully feared by *Terrify*.
- - Fiddlesticks has a special icon for this ability.
- Crowstorm will not fear enemy champions if cast while they have vision of **Fiddlesticks**, even if **Fiddlesticks** is pretending to be an Effigy.
