### Passive: A Harmless Scarecrow

**Innate:** **Fiddlesticks** has a *Scarecrow Effigy* in place of a trinket, which will imitate **Fiddlesticks**. **Fiddlesticks** can impersonate an effigy by remaining idle out of combat.

*After level 6, placing an effigy will also summon a Sweeper Drone over the area for a few seconds.*

**Innate - Scarecrow Effigy:** **Fiddlesticks** begins the game with an exclusive *Scarecrow Effigy*, which permanently occupies the trinket slot. **Fiddlesticks** can pretend to be an *Effigy* by standing still and not acting nor being acted upon for 2 seconds, during which it adjusts its body into a scarecrow and extends its arm out to expose its lantern. **Innate - A Harmless Scarecrow:** From *experience* 6 onward, placing an Effigy also summons a Sweeper Drone at the location for 6 seconds.
