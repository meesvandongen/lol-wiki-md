### Passive: Pix, Faerie Companion

**Innate:** **Lulu** is assisted by **Pix**, whom follows her around. Whenever **Lulu** basic attacks, **Pix** fires 3 bolts that deal magic damage to the first enemy hit.

*Casting **Help, Pix!** on an ally champion transfers **Pix** to them, to which he fires with their basic attacks.*

**Innate:** **Lulu** is assisted by *Pix*, an untargetable faerie, who follows her around and fires a barrage of 3 bolts towards her target whenever she uses a basic attack on-attack. Each bolt deals 5 to 39 (+ 5% AP) magic damage to the first enemy it collides with, for a total of 5×3 to 39×3 (+ 15% AP) on hitting a single target with all three bolts. Casting **Help, Pix!** on an ally champion transfers 'Pix's help to them, causing him to fire the bolts dependent on their basic attacks. *Pix snaps back to **Lulu** if she is too far away.*

## Trivia

- Lulu is the first champion to:
  - Be announced directly through an "Art Spotlight".
  - Be available for play on the PBE before her official release.
  - Have her own login screen and dedicated theme.
- Lulu was the last champion to release with 2 skins alongside her Classic one.
- *Whimsy*, *Help, Pix!*, *Lamb's Respite*, *Tempered Fate* and *Time Warp* are the only abilities in the game that can affect themselves, allies, and enemies.
- Lulu's abilities grant buffs and debuffs depending if her target is an ally or an enemy.
  - Using *Help, Pix!* on an enemy will grant them a debuff reading 'Hey! Listen!' referencing from The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
  - In chat, the enemy player's character name is replaced by "Harmless Critter" while they are polymorph by *Whimsy*.
    - Before V11.1, this changed name would range along a number of possible ones, depending on Lulu's skin: Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu and Lulu.
