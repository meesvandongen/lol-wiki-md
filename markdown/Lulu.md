### Passive: Pix, Faerie Companion

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|------:|
| **Width** | 60 (Bolt missile width) units |
| **Speed** | 900 - 2600 (Bolt missile speed, 5200 acceleration; reaching maximum speed within 0.33 seconds) units/second |
| **Targeting** | Passive |
| **Affects** | Self |
| **Damage Type** | Magic |
| **Spell Effects** | default |
| **Projectile** | True |
| **Parry** | False |
| **Leash Range** | 1600 (Pix' leash range when moving freely) / 2050 (Pix' leash range when on another unit (Estimated)) |

**INNATE:** **Lulu** is assisted by *Pix*, an untargetable faerie, who follows her around and fires a barrage of 3 bolts towards her target whenever she uses a basic attack on-attack. Each bolt deals 5 to 39 (+ 5% AP) magic damage to the first enemy it collides with, for a total of 5×3 to 39×3 (+ 15% AP) on hitting a single target with all three bolts.

Casting *Help, Pix!* on an ally champion transfers *Pix*’s help to them, causing him to fire the bolts dependent on their basic attacks.

*Pix snaps back to **Lulu** if she is too far away.*

**Notes:**

- *Pix*’s bolts do not fire alongside abilities that apply on-hit effects (e.g. Runaan's Hurricane).
- *Pix* does not fire if the target dies.
- *Pix* fires from his own location and not from the unit he is following.
- When on an ally champion, *Pix*’s damage respects enchantment redirection.
- The empowered attack does not trigger against structures nor wards.
  - Neither of them will intercept *Pix*’s bolts.

## Trivia

- Lulu is the first champion to:
  - Be announced directly through an "Art Spotlight".
  - Be available for play on the PBE before her official release.
  - Have her own login screen and dedicated theme.
- Lulu was the last champion to release with 2 skins alongside her Classic one.
- Whimsy, Help, Pix!, Lamb's Respite, Tempered Fate and Time Warp are the only abilities in the game that can affect themselves, allies, and enemies.
- Lulu's abilities grant buffs and debuffs depending if her target is an ally or an enemy.
  - Using Help, Pix! on an enemy will grant them a debuff reading 'Hey! Listen!' referencing from The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
  - In chat, the enemy player's character name is replaced by "Harmless Critter" while they are polymorphed by Whimsy.
    - Before V11.1 (Estimated, change was not documented in patch notes), this changed name would range along a number of possible ones, depending on Lulu's skin: Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu, Lulu and Lulu.
