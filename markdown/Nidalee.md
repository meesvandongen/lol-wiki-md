## Trivia

- Nidalee is the first champion to have more than 4 active abilities.
- Nidalee, Elise, Gnar, Jayce, and Kayle are the only champions to be conditionally considered both ranged and melee.
- Nidalee was the first champion to feature a transformation ability (e.g. Elise, Gnar Shyvana, Swain).
- Nidalee has four different running animations (human run, human brush sprint, cougar run, cougar brush sprint).
- Pounce was the first ability to feature a static cooldown (was removed in V1.0.0.124).
- Nidalee used to have a hidden passive where nearby allied champions gain 5 **bonus** experience every 5 seconds if Nidalee is Cougar (slang) (a double-entendre).
- In older versions of the game, Javelin Toss was the highest-damage single-hit spell in the game.
- Nidalee was deemed overpowered in the Ultra Rapid Fire game mode (2014 edition) and was ultimately disabled in non-custom games.
- After her visual rework, her feline forms received morphological features from cougars' Pantherinae, like mane, higher shoulders than haunches, long skull & jaws, & occasionally long fangs of Machairodontinae and other Barbourofelidae Nimravidae.
- Nidalee is one of the first champions to have one skin for each of the three big annual events of the year: Lunar Revel, Harrowing, Snowdown Showdown. She received her Nidalee skin alongside Katarina’s in Warring Kingdoms 2015.md).
- Given that she is a feral child, it is unknown how Nidalee could have learnt to speak languages fluently; since in the real world, feral humans who learn their first language Critical Period never master the linguistic aspects of phonology, morphology, and syntax.
