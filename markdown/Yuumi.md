## Trivia

- Due to much of her intended gameplay consisting of not moving of her own volition like all other champions do and exclusively boosting the power of other champions, **Yuumi** has jokingly been referred to as an "item": an enhancement that merely empowers champions rather than a "full champion" herself. Riot have officially (and humorously) acknowledged this twice so far, by implementing the Teamfight Tactics item **Yuumi* and the Swarm weapon drone ccibYuumiBot*.
- The icon for *You and Me!* is reused for the Teamfight Tactics item *Yuumi*, which is named after her.
