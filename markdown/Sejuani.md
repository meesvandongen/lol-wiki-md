## Trivia

- She is referenced in Assassin's Creed Valhalla.
- Sejuani is currently the champion with the shortest time between their initial release and a full visual game play update, at 1 year and 3 months.
- Sejuani was the first champion released in 2012.
- In Sejuani's Art Spotlight, the artist names the layer that has the boar on it "Piggles". A screenshot can be seen here.
- If **Ashe**, **Lissandra**, and/or Sejuani are played on opposing teams, Battle for Freljord will trigger. This in-game quest consists in one killing the other to earn the title of 'Queen of Freljord' (complete with a floating ice crown floating above their heads) referencing the civil war taking place there, between the three tribes each lead.
- Sejuani, **Corki**, **Kled**, **Nunu**, **Rell**, and **Rumble** are the only mounted champions.
- Sejuani, **Karma**, **Lee Sin**, and **Trundle** are the only champions to feature a 'traditional' skin representing them before their visual updates (hers was conceived after an unreleased 'Queen' skin).
- ***Bristle*** animations were recycled for the Bristle monster from Odyssey: Extraction, a rotating game mode.
